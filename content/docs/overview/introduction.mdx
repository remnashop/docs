Remnashop is a ready-made platform for selling VPN subscriptions on top of
[Remnawave](https://github.com/remnawave). Customers buy and renew their subscription in the
Telegram bot or the web cabinet, and you run everything from the dashboard. The platform is free,
open source under the MIT license, and runs on your own server with Docker.

The Telegram bot and the web cabinet work together or on their own. Remnashop takes no commission on sales — you only pay your payment
gateway. All data stays in your database: the bot token, payment gateway keys and OAuth
secrets are encrypted.

You need a running Remnawave panel and a server with Docker — see the
[requirements](/docs/getting-started/requirements).

<Accordions title="Features" description="What Remnashop can do" icon="LayoutGrid">

<Accordion title="Analytics" icon="ChartColumn">
- Home page: revenue for a period, subscription state, online users and the latest payments.
- Nodes, countries and client apps.
- Key metrics compared with the previous period: revenue, MRR, ARPPU, new payers, conversion and churn.
- New customer funnel: sign-up, VPN connection, first and repeat payment — also by source.
- Revenue by day, week and month, split into new subscriptions, renewals and plan changes, by plan and by duration; average order value and refunds.
- Payment success rate per gateway.
- LTV: mean and median, distribution, by first plan and by source, top payers.
- Retention: the curve and cohorts by month of first payment, monthly churn, subscriptions expiring in the next 30 days.
- Trial: conversion to payment, the day customers buy on and what they pick after the trial.
- Acquisition sources, referral program payback and top referrers.
- Promo code effectiveness and activations by reward type.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Plans" icon="Package">
- Traffic limits, device limits, both or unlimited.
- Any durations; reorder plans by dragging.
- Availability: everyone, new, existing, invited, a list of Telegram IDs and emails, or by direct link only.
- Internal and external squads per plan.
- A plan discount with start and end dates.
- Trial plans — paid or free.
- Add-ons on top of a subscription: extra traffic and devices.
- The use of bonus days and promo codes can be allowed or blocked per plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Prices and currencies" icon="Coins">
- Rubles, US dollars and Telegram Stars.
- An anchor currency with conversion rates, rounding and minimum amounts.
- A price matrix with per-currency overrides for plans and add-ons.
- Add-on pricing per day and per GB.
- Bulk price adjustment with a preview of the changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Subscriptions" icon="Layers">
- Purchase, renewal and plan change; a limit on active subscriptions per user, or no limit.
- Data sync with Remnawave.
- Grant, change plan, enable and disable, reset traffic, reissue the link, squads and delete.
- Users can reset their devices (one at a time or all at once) and their subscription link themselves, with a configurable interval between resets.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Promo codes" icon="Ticket">
- Rewards: extra days, traffic or devices, a free subscription, a personal discount or a purchase discount.
- Expiry, activation limit and reusable codes.
- Availability: everyone, new, existing, invited or a list; limited to selected plans.
- Summary: active promo codes, activations, granted days and subscriptions, discounted orders.
- Promo code card: uses against the limit and the list of users who activated it.
- Analytics: activation-to-payment conversion, revenue from those who activated and the amount given away in discounts.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Discounts and loyalty" icon="BadgePercent">
- Personal discount, one-time purchase discount and loyalty discount — the largest one applies.
- A plan discount stacks with the personal one: the personal discount is taken from the already reduced price.
- Loyalty program: tiers by total paid, an accounting window in months, excluding individual customers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Referral program" icon="Share2">
- Up to three levels in the chain.
- Rewards: bonus days for renewing a subscription, or points — fixed or a percentage of the payment.
- Accrual on the first payment or on every payment.
- A reward for the invitee on sign-up through the link.
- Points withdrawal requests, adjusting points and bonus days.
- One referral code with links to the bot and to the web cabinet; the code can be reset.
- Revenue from invitees for each referrer.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Payment systems" icon="CreditCard">
- Gateways: Telegram Stars, YooKassa, YooMoney, Cryptomus, Heleket, CryptoPay (CryptoBot), FreeKassa, MulenPay, PayMaster, Platega, RoboKassa, UrlPay, WATA, Valutix, RollyPay, AntiloPay, PayPalych, RioPay, AuraPay, SeverPay, Lava, CloudPayments, PayPear.
- Enabling, display order and test payments.
- Manually fulfilling a subscription for an unpaid transaction.
- Waitlist: with payments turned off, customers are notified once they are back on.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Transactions" icon="Receipt">
- The transaction list with search and filters by status, gateway, purchase type and date.
- A summary of the selected transactions.
- A transaction card with all its details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Broadcasts" icon="Megaphone">
- Audience by filters — subscription, plans, expiry, payments, language, source, sign-up date — or selected customers.
- Photos, videos, GIFs and documents; buttons with a link, a promo code or a jump to the menu.
- Posting to a Telegram channel.
- Preview, test send and scheduled send.
- Cancel, retry failed sends and recall sent messages.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bot builder" icon="LayoutList">
- Window editor: content, buttons and navigation, custom windows.
- Action, navigation and link buttons in three styles.
- Banners per window and language.
- Telegram premium emoji packs.
- Bot commands and their order.
- Requirements before use: accept the rules, join a channel.
- Window import and export.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Languages" icon="Languages">
- Built-in Russian and English translations; more languages can be added.
- A text editor in the dashboard: messages, notifications and errors.
- Translation import, export and reset.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Web cabinet" icon="Globe">
- Opens in a browser or as a Telegram Mini App.
- Sign-in with email and password or through Telegram, Yandex and Google.
- Purchase and renewal, promo codes, devices, the referral program and the profile.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Access and security" icon="ShieldCheck">
- Modes: open to everyone, invite-only or closed for maintenance; sign-up and payments toggled separately.
- Dashboard sign-in with a password, a passkey, Telegram, GitHub or OpenID Connect (SSO).
- Administrators with a configurable set of permissions.
- IP allowlist, active sessions, alerts on failed sign-ins and suspected session theft.
- API tokens with the permissions and lifetime you choose.
- API docs (Swagger and ReDoc) open from the dashboard with a one-time ticket.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Users" icon="UserPen">
- User card: subscriptions, transactions, referrals and promo codes.
- Block, delete, discounts, Telegram ID binding and ending sessions.
- Account merging.
- Search by name, username and Telegram ID.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Blacklist" icon="Ban">
- Blocking by Telegram ID and email.
- External lists with automatic sync.
- Matches among registered users and bulk unblocking.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Ad links" icon="Link2">
- One link works for both the bot and the web cabinet; links and QR codes for each channel.
- A custom or generated code, placement cost in rubles, dollars or Stars, a note.
- Stats: sign-ups, purchases, conversion, revenue, spend, CPA and ROI.
- Sign-ups by day, split into the bot and the web cabinet.
- The list of attributed users, marked by whether they bought.
- Pausing a link while keeping its stats.
- The user card shows which link the user came from.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notifications" icon="Bell">
- "Needs attention" on the home page: offline nodes, payment errors, failed backups, paid but unfulfilled subscriptions, stuck payments.
- Channels: the dashboard, web push and Telegram.
- Each administrator chooses which notifications to get and where.
- Event log.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Maintenance" icon="Wrench">
- Backups of the database and media files.
- A schedule with copy rotation, and manual runs.
- Downloading and deleting finished copies in the dashboard.
- Restoring a backup through the CLI.
- Emergency access through the CLI: resetting owner access and re-enabling password sign-in.
- Health of the app processes and the server.
- Telegram bot status and logs, a restart from the dashboard, a proxy for reaching Telegram.
- Prometheus metrics on the api and worker processes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Appearance" icon="Palette">
- Brand name, accent colour, logo and favicon.
- A background shader with its own palette, which can be turned off.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
